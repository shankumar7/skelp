The IPL-style player auction you play with your friends. Auctions run fully offline.

**Download `skelp.apk` below**, open it on your Android phone (7.0 or newer) and allow *Install unknown apps*.

### New in 1.4
- **Continue with Google.** Sign in with your Google account in two taps, on the sign-in and registration screens. First-time Google players pick an avatar, then go straight in. Google accounts are deleted the same way, by confirming with Google.

### From 1.3
- Accounts with email confirmation and password reset, sent by Firebase
- Profile, stats and recent auctions backed up and synced across phones; guests still work without an account
- Results from auctions played offline upload the next time the phone is online
- Delete your account (and its backed-up data) from the dashboard

> **Coming from 1.2 or earlier?** Since 1.3 Skelp!'s Android ID is `com.classcom.skelp`, so it installs as a new app. Uninstall the old Skelp! afterwards.

### Good to know
- One phone hosts, the rest join over the same Wi-Fi or hotspot; auctions never need the internet
- The host phone must keep Skelp! open during an auction
- The APK is signed with a debug key, which is fine for sharing the file directly
