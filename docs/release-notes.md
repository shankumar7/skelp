The IPL-style player auction you play with your friends. Auctions run fully offline.

**Download `skelp.apk` below**, open it on your Android phone (7.0 or newer) and allow *Install unknown apps*.

> **Installs as a new app.** From 1.3 Skelp!'s Android ID is `com.classcom.skelp`, so it will not replace an older Skelp!. Install it, then uninstall the old one. Profiles from older versions were stored only on that phone and do not carry over.

### New in 1.3
- **Accounts.** Sign up with your email to back up your profile, stats and recent auctions, and sign in on any phone. Guests still work without an account.
- **Email confirmation and password reset**, sent by Firebase. *Forgot password?* is on the sign-in screen.
- **Offline-friendly sync.** Results from auctions played without internet are uploaded the next time the phone is online.
- **Delete your account** (and its backed-up data) from the dashboard at any time.
- Fixed: the "next player in…" countdown kept running on screen while the host had paused the auction.
- Phones joined to a hotspot without internet no longer lose internet access for the rest of the app.

### From 1.2.1
- Fixed the app closing on launch for signed-in players who were not on Wi-Fi
- Recovery screen instead of a crash if anything unexpected goes wrong

### Good to know
- One phone hosts, the rest join over the same Wi-Fi or hotspot; auctions never need the internet
- The host phone must keep Skelp! open during an auction
- The APK is signed with a debug key, which is fine for sharing the file directly
