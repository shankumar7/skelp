The IPL-style player auction you play with your friends, fully offline.

**Download `skelp.apk` below**, open it on your Android phone (7.0 or newer) and allow *Install unknown apps*.

### What's new in 1.2
- **Fixed: phones could not connect over a hotspot.** Connections are now bound to Wi-Fi (a hotspot has no internet, so Android was sending them over mobile data), and searching for the host no longer blocks other connections
- **Join by QR code:** the host's lobby shows a QR code; friends tap *Scan QR*
- **Hotspot guide:** step-by-step instructions in the lobby, a Wi-Fi status line on the dashboard, and one-tap shortcuts to hotspot and Wi-Fi settings

### How to play together
1. One person turns on their mobile hotspot (or everyone joins the same Wi-Fi)
2. The others connect to it from their Wi-Fi settings
3. Host taps *Host your own arena*; friends tap *Scan QR* and point at the host's screen

### Good to know
- No internet needed, and mobile data can stay off
- The host phone must keep Skelp! open during an auction
- The camera is used only to read the QR code
- The APK is signed with a debug key, which is fine for sharing the file directly
