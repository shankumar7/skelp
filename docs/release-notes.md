The IPL-style player auction you play with your friends, fully offline.

**Download `skelp.apk` below**, open it on your Android phone (7.0 or newer) and allow *Install unknown apps*.

### What's new in 1.1
- Sound effects and haptics: bid blips, a ticking last five seconds, the gavel on *Sold*, with a mute button
- New warm clay look with a countdown bar and price animation
- Emoji reactions, outbid alerts, an *Up next* strip and a *Sold* feed
- Player pool browser with search and role filters
- Steal of the auction and one-tap sharing on the results screen
- 85 of 137 players now have a photo
- APK is 26 MB (was 46 MB)
- Faster arena screen, instant resync after the app returns from the background
- Stricter checks on everything received from other phones; fewer permissions

### Good to know
- No internet needed: one phone hosts, the rest join over the same Wi-Fi or hotspot
- The host phone must keep Skelp! open during an auction
- The APK is signed with a debug key, which is fine for sharing the file directly
