The IPL-style player auction you play with your friends — offline over Wi-Fi or a hotspot, or online with a code.

**Download `skelp.apk` below**, open it on your Android phone (7.0 or newer) and allow *Install unknown apps*.

> **Have 1.5?** Open Skelp! and it offers this update itself (or use *About → Check for updates*).
> **Have 1.4 or earlier?** Uninstall the old Skelp! first, then install this one. This is a one-time step.

### New in 1.8
- **Players come up in sets**, like the real auction: the marquee set first, then Batters Set 1, All-rounders Set 1, Wicket-keepers Set 1, Bowlers Set 1, then each Set 2 and so on. The most popular players are in the earlier sets. The arena shows the set and the player's place in it, and the *Players* tab is grouped by set.
- **Shortlist.** Star players in the pool or in the arena; you get an alert the moment one of them comes up.
- **Every purse at a glance.** A strip under the bid shows each team's purse and squad size, who is leading and who has passed.
- **Safe maximum bid.** The arena tells you the most you can bid and still fill your eleven.
- **Rejoin.** If the app closes mid-auction, the dashboard offers your arena back.
- **Team crests.** Each franchise has its own crest in the team colours (original designs, not the official logos, which are trademarks).

### From 1.7
- **Run a real IPL team.** Everyone picks one of the ten franchises in the lobby, and teams are shown by their name (CSK, MI, RCB…) and colour instead of the player's name.
- **Players list in the arena.** A new *Players* tab shows every player in the auction: on the block, still to come, sold (to whom and for how much) and unsold.

### From 1.6
- **Play online.** Host an arena for friends anywhere: choose *Anywhere • Online* when hosting, share the six-character code (or the invite message), and friends type it in the arena code box. The host's phone still runs the auction; Firebase only relays the messages.
- No account needed for online play — guests work too.
- Wi-Fi and hotspot play is unchanged and still needs no internet.

### From 1.5
- **In-app updates.** Skelp! checks for a new version when it opens (and from *About → Check for updates*), shows what is new, downloads it and opens Android's installer.
- **Right to Match and retentions.** Optionally give every team a squad from "last season": keep up to three players at fixed prices before the auction, and use RTM cards to take a former player back at the winning bid.
- **Auctioneer mode.** Put the auction on a tablet or a spare phone as the big screen while everyone bids from their own phone.
- **Custom player pools.** Indian-only or overseas-only auctions, and up to 20 players of your own.
- **Undo.** The host can take back the latest bid.
- **Smarter bots.** Six bidding styles, squads balanced by role, jump bids, and they use retentions and RTM cards.
- **Auction history.** Every finished auction is saved on the phone with its full squads.
- **Share as a picture.** Send the final table as an image.
- **Edit your profile.** Change your name and avatar any time.
- **Dark mode** and a refreshed, calmer clay design throughout.
- **Tablets and landscape.** Screens use the extra width.
- **About the developer, About the app**, and *Affiliated with ClassCom*.
- Anonymous crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) to help fix bugs.

### Good to know
- Nearby: one phone hosts, the rest join over the same Wi-Fi or hotspot; no internet needed
- Online: everyone needs the internet, and the host shares the arena code
- Everyone in an arena needs the same version
- The host phone must keep Skelp! open during an auction
